## 5. Judgment Layer (human-written, about five lines)

During this Sprint, we learned about different frameworks and their tradeoffs for our project. We compared Streamlit with the combination of React and FastAPI. It turned out that although Streamlit makes it easier to build a quick prototype, FastAPI and React provides more flexibility in designing the UI and building a strong foundation for future development. As a result, after contemplating the pros and cons between different frameworks, our team decided to use FastAPI and React as the front/backend for our project.
