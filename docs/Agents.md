# TherapistFlow: a burnout prevention scheduler for therapists and supervisors

## Project Overview
TherapistFlow is a scheduling and wellness management tool for mental health counselors and therapists. It is designed primarily for clinicians to manage their workloads by logging client sessions, tracking clinician mood, and addressing the risk level of professional burnout. 

The projected audience for TherapistFlow are individual clinicians, supervisors, and agencies that employ them. The overall goal is to create a sustainable workflow for mental health professionals bu designing balanced habits and workflow.
